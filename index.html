Poster Work in progress

Full-wave and machine-learning modelling of cylindrical fiber Bragg gratings

Gökay Gökcan, Hasan Oğuz
Poster presentation · Turkish Physical Society (TFD)

A study of fiber Bragg gratings with high index contrast. The cylindrical structure is simulated by solving Maxwell's equations directly, using two independent solvers (FDTD with MEEP and eigenmode analysis with MPB), and the simulation results are used to train a machine-learning surrogate model that predicts the grating's response for new designs.
